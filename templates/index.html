CROP RECOMMENDATION SYSTEM

An intelligent agricultural decision support website that assists farmers, agricultural students, and researchers in selecting the most suitable crop for cultivation based on soil nutrients and local weather conditions.

Get Recommendation

🧪 Soil Nutrient Analysis

Takes into account essential primary soil nutrients: Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), and Potassium (K), which determine plant growth and soil fertility.

🌦️ Climate Factors

Considers temperature, atmospheric humidity, and rainfall to ensure the crop can thrive in the prevailing climatic conditions.

🤖 Machine Learning Model

Uses a trained Random Forest classification algorithm to analyze the multi-dimensional parameter set and suggest the optimal crop.

Simple 3-Step Process

Quick and straightforward guidance for your field

  1. Enter Soil & Weather Values: Provide values for N, P, K, pH, temperature, humidity, and rainfall.
  2. ML Processing: The Random Forest model evaluates the values against historical agronomic patterns.
  3. Instant Crop Advice: Receive an immediate recommendation of the most suitable crop for optimal yield.
